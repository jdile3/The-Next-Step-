# THE NEXT STEP: MONEY
> *"Meet people where they are. Give them the next step."*

A realistic, zero-judgment personal cash flow tool. Built for real paychecks, practical spending clarity, and financial rebuilding.

---

## 🧭 About The Project & Founder's Statement

This app started out of raw necessity as a personal workbook. It wasn't built to compete with big corporate finance platforms or sell user data to credit card companies; it was built because everyday people need a straightforward tool that gives total clarity on cash flow without confusion, shame, or surveillance.

> **"I want absolutely nothing to do with users' data. I am simply selling a screen full of code that people can use and interact with on their own devices. That's it."** — *Founder & Creator*

### Core Principles
1. **Zero Judgment**: No lectures, no guilt trips, and no unsolicited advice. Money is a tool for living life.
2. **Essentials First**: Account for critical obligations (housing, utilities, transportation, food) during the active pay period first. What remains is your actual **Free-to-Spend** amount.
3. **Honest Clarity**: A neutral, numbers-first look at spending patterns and habit burn rates so you know your limits before you run into trouble.
4. **100% Local & Private**: All financial data stays strictly on your personal device (`localStorage`). No bank scrapers, no third-party tracking, and no external databases.
5. **Zero User-Billed APIs**: Core features run locally without attaching paid external AI keys or generating monthly token bills.

## ⚡ Core Engine & Features

### 1. Active Pay-Period Engine
Tracks finances based on actual paydays rather than arbitrary calendar months:
- Supports Weekly, Bi-Weekly, and Monthly schedules.
- Automatically calculates active dates and days remaining until next deposit.
- Computes: `Available Cash - Bills Due This Period - Goal Allocations = Free to Spend`.

### 2. Reality Check & Leak Mirror (Insights)
- **Habit Burn Tracker**: Tag any custom category (e.g., Entertainment, Dining Out, Casino, Fast Food) to view total pay-period burn, monthly burn, and projected 12-month annualized cost with zero judgment.
- **The Cliff Line (Walk-Away Limit)**: Calculates the exact dollar threshold you can spend tonight before encroaching on upcoming survival bill obligations.
- **Life Hours Traded**: Translates expense totals into hours worked on the clock and 8-hour work shifts based on your hourly take-home wage.

### 3. Synchronized Calendar & Bill Ledger
- Monthly calendar grid highlighting bill due dates and paydays.
- Filtered pay-period ledger showing only bills due before the next check.
- One-tap "Mark Paid" toggle that automatically updates balances and logs transactions.

### 4. Smart Offline CSV Machine
- Simple column mapper for bank statement imports with rotating 3-sample row preview.
- Automatically detects recurring charges and suggests recurring bill entries.
- Filters out duplicate and cancelled entries.

### 5. On-Device Receipt Scanner
- Camera/photo import with local processing running on-device for $0.00 compute cost.

### 6. Data Controls
- Complete JSON export and import for seamless manual backups.
- Clean CSV ledger export for spreadsheets.
